**4. Synthesis**
**Winner: A.**
- **Growth:** A has the largest association with second purchase (81.6% vs 40.5%).
- **Finance:** A has no incentive, so no discount leakage.
- **Operations:** A meets Doc D without packaging redesign.
- **Sustainability:** pause/skip stops refills arriving early (Doc B).

**Strongest case against:** the gap may come from who was shown the offer, not the offer itself. Offer assignment and acceptance are not in evidence. The pilot should therefore randomize choose-cadence against no offer. Eligible population, total spend and break-even are not in evidence, because volume and margins are missing.

**Losing options:**
- **B:** folded in as a guardrail: verify and recover packaging issues (average ≤ INR 65) before any plan prompt.
- **C:** kept as a companion reminder at the cadence the customer chose, with the incentive dropped.
- **Packaging redesign:** stays a later-stage option under Doc D.
